# Reviewing an organization's past event

An organization records an event that already happened with up to 100
volunteers, each with their own proposed hours and XP. It reaches **Past
events** as one row in the Waiting tab. Open it to see everyone on it.

- **Approve all** credits every participant still waiting with the
  organization's figures. The confirmation states how many people and how many
  hours and XP in total. It is all or none: if one account can no longer be
  credited (blocked, or merged into another participant), nobody is approved
  and the message says so — reject that person below, then approve the rest.
- **Reject all** turns down everyone still waiting with one reason the
  organization reads.
- Once anyone on the event is decided, the two buttons read **Approve
  remaining** and **Reject remaining** and act only on the people still
  waiting.
- **Approve** or **Reject** on a row decides one person; the dialog title names
  them. Approve opens the
  hours and XP so a wrong figure can be corrected first; the row then says the
  amounts were corrected.

Decisions already made stay on the page with their badge and reason. The event
moves to the Reviewed tab once nobody waits, with one state: approved, partly
approved, rejected or withdrawn.

A participant may have no earlier event with the organization; that is valid
for past events. Lookup exposes only public identity fields, and pending
participation is never activity or credit. Approved participation appears in
the volunteer's history and profile, and the event is listed under Past events
in the volunteer app's Opportunities.

Corrections and removals of one volunteer's recorded past event are listed
under the events on the Waiting tab and decided one at a time.

See [the backend workflow](../../../v-backend/docs/plans/ORG_HISTORICAL_EVENTS.md)
for the API, the all-or-none transaction and verification.
